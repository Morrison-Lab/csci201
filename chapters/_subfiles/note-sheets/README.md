# Exam note sheet subfiles

These are the sections of the exam note sheets in `chapters/note-sheets/`,
one topic per file.
Each sheet includes the topics its exam covers,
and the final's sheet includes them all,
so a change to a topic reaches every sheet that uses it.

Students get these sheets ahead of time and with each exam,
in place of notes of their own.
A sheet states definitions, laws and notation,
which the exams ask students to apply.
It leaves out a result when an exam item asks for that result itself,
so that the sheet does not answer the item:

- no sizes of power sets or Cartesian products (Midterm 1 and the final);
- no sum of the first $n$ odd numbers (Midterms 1 and 2 and the final);
- no growth-rate hierarchy, that is, which of $\log n$, $n^k$ and $2^n$ is big-O of which (Midterm 2 and the final);
- no handshake lemma and no condition for an Euler circuit or trail (Midterm 3 and the final);
- no complement construction for a DFA (Midterm 3 and the final);
- no recipe for what each proof method assumes (every sheet).

The final's sheet is the three midterm sheets' tables together:
it includes the same fragments,
so each leave-out applies to it too.

Before adding a result to a sheet,
check every exam item that the sheet's exam includes.

Keep this note here rather than in an HTML comment on a page:
a comment on the page reaches the published HTML.
